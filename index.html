THE SHOOTER

SHOOT EVERYTHING.
WASTE NOTHING.

Based in Portland, OR. I shoot whatever doesn't run fast enough — mountain ridges, back alleys, fog-soaked coastlines, city walls that shouldn't be beautiful but are. Seven years with a camera. Three with film. Prints shipped in cardboard tubes from my living room.

Available for editorial, landscape, and documentary work. I don't shoot weddings. I do shoot things that feel like the end of the world in the best possible way.
